Store Management

Store Management is an offline inventory and sales management application designed for small businesses. It helps business owners manage products, inventory, customers, suppliers, orders, expenses, and business reports efficiently without requiring a constant internet connection.

Features

  • Inventory Management
  • Sales and Purchase Tracking
  • Customer and Supplier Management
  • Expense Tracking
  • Reports and Analytics
  • Google Drive Backup and Restore

Google Drive Backup

Store Management allows users to back up and restore their application data using Google Drive.

Backups are stored securely in the application's private Google Drive App Data Folder, which is hidden from the user's normal Google Drive files.

The application only accesses backup files that it creates and manages. It cannot view, modify, or access other files stored in your Google Drive.
